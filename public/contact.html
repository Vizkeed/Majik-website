Questions, answered

Quick answers before you message. Not here? Just ask us.

How do I place an order?
Add dishes to your order on the menu page and send it on WhatsApp. It arrives as a ready-written message and we confirm within minutes. You can also call us.
When are you open?
Monday to Saturday 9am to 9pm, and Sunday 12pm to 8pm.
How early should I order a cake or catering?
Cakes and catering orders are best placed 48 hours ahead. For large events, message us as early as you can.
Do you cater outside Apapa?
Yes. Our catering covers Lagos, including cooking, delivery, setup and service.
Do you deliver everyday meals?
Choose delivery when you send your order and we confirm the area and fee with you on WhatsApp.
Where exactly are you?
111 Oba Olu-Iwa Road (also 111 Kirikiri Road), near Wilmer Bus Stop, Olodi, Apapa, Lagos.
